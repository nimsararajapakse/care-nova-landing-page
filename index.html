Care that feels like family, for the people who raised us.

Sri Lanka is ageing fast. Care at home needs to catch up.

More people need daily support, while fewer working-age relatives are at home to give it. Many families now look for a paid helper, often for the first time.

Today that search runs on referrals, classified ads and agencies, with no clear way to see who is qualified or what they can handle.

Finding a helper shouldn't be guesswork

You're trusting someone with a person you love. You should know exactly who they are and what they can do before they arrive.

How families find help today

Word of mouth, ikman ads and private agencies.

  • Qualifications you can't checkA recommendation is all you have to go on.
  • No match for the conditionCaring for dementia or recovery after a stroke needs different skills.
  • Little visibility once care startsNo daily record and no quick way to raise an alarm.
  • Starting again when plans fall throughIf a helper can't come, the search begins from zero.

With CareNova

One app to find, book and stay in touch with your relative's care.

  • Clear helper profilesQualifications, experience, skills and the conditions each helper handles.
  • Condition-specific matchingHelpers ranked by how well they suit your relative's needs.
  • Daily care logs and an SOS buttonSee what happened each day, and get an alert with location in an emergency.
  • Backup helpers suggested for youIf a booking falls through, suitable replacements are ready.

Questions families ask

Can't find what you're looking for? Join the waitlist and we'll keep you posted as we build.

Who is CareNova for?

Families in Sri Lanka who need a paid helper for an elderly or ill relative, and helpers who want to offer their care skills. Hospitals and doctors may join in a later stage.

How are helpers checked?

Helpers upload their qualifications and references when they build a profile, and this is shown clearly to families. CareNova doesn't carry out its own background checks, so we recommend reading each profile and its reviews before you book.

How does the matching work?

You describe your relative's condition and needs. CareNova compares them with each helper's experience, skills, training badges, language and location, then ranks the helpers who fit best.

What happens if my helper can't come?

CareNova suggests suitable backup helpers so you can arrange cover quickly, without starting your search again.

Which languages does the app support?

Sinhala, Tamil and English. You can switch at any time, and families and helpers can each use the language they prefer.

How do payments work?

You pay for a booking inside the app through a secure payment provider. CareNova takes a small commission on each booking to run the service.
